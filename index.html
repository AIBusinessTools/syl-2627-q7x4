McCombs courses in artificial intelligence

This page lists courses at the McCombs School of Business on artificial intelligence and links each to its syllabus where one is available. Several are listed in the course schedule under a generic title such as "Topics in Management Information Systems"; for those, this page shows the course's own title, from its syllabus where one is posted and from the course schedule's topic title otherwise. Several course numbers, such as MIS 372T, MIS 382N, and MKT 382, are topics numbers that more than one course uses, so the same number can appear on more than one row.
